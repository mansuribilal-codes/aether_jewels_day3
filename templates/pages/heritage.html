Centennial Sovereign Legacy

The Lore of Celestial Craftsmanship

Nearly a century of uniting India’s royal gemstone provenance with Parisian haute joaillerie architecture.

1928 • The Genesis

The Royal Vaults of Jaipur

Founded in 1928 under royal patronage in Rajasthan, Aether Jewels originated as private master goldsmiths to sovereign maharajas. Tasked with crafting coronation crowns, turban kalgis, and ceremonial necklaces, our atelier perfected the synthesis of Syndicate Polki diamonds and antique basra pearls.

Each generation guarded the proprietary metallurgical secrets of sovereign champagne gold and 24-facet starlight cutting.

1964 • The Paris Rendezvous

Haute Joaillerie Fusion

In the mid-20th century, the second generation of Aether jewelers established collaborative benches at Place Vendôme in Paris. This cross-cultural dialogue introduced high-tensile platinum wirework and French articulated hinges into the majestic weight of Indian royal jewellery.

The result was wearable high jewellery: monumental in gemstone scale, yet as light and fluid as liquid silk upon the collarbone.

Present • The Celestial Vision

Ethical Sourcing & Sovereign Hallmarking

Today, Aether Jewels operates as an exclusive high jewellery atelier catering to discerning global connoisseurs. Every diamond is certified by GIA, strictly 100% compliant with the Kimberley Process, and set in 100% recycled 950 Sovereign Platinum or BIS 916 Hallmarked Gold.
