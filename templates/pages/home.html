Sovereign Metier

The Art of Celestial Goldsmithing

Every jewel traverses an uncompromising 800-hour journey across four sovereign milestones.

01
Ethical Provenance

Cosmic Gemstone Sourcing

Our master gemologists scour historic riverbeds and royal private vaults to procure stones of astronomical rarity: unheated Kashmir sapphires with natural silk rutile, D-Flawless Golconda Type IIa diamonds, and Muzo emeralds of vivid saturation.

100% Kimberley Process & Provenance Ledger
02
4th Generation Ateliers

800-Hour Hand Forging

No 3D printing or cast assembly can emulate the acoustic and tactile warmth of hand-forged 950 Sovereign Platinum and 18k Champagne Gold. Our fourth-generation smiths in Jaipur and Mumbai hammer and calibrate metal to sub-millimeter tolerances.

Hand-Forged Ingot Metallurgy
03
Optical Precision

40x Micro-Pavé Starlight Alignment

Every accent diamond is faceted under 40x stereoscopic magnification. Stones are set using micro-bead prongs aligned to astronomical golden ratios, allowing light to cascade across the piece with zero optical dark spots.

40x Stereoscopic Precision
04
Sovereign Certification

Dual GIA & BIS 916 Sovereign Hallmark

Prior to entering the client vault, each masterpiece receives dual international certification from the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) and the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS 916), accompanied by an encrypted provenance ledger.

Lifetime Authentication Registry
